About The Bull Print

Demystifying Dalal Street, global macroeconomics, and decentralized finance through forensic balance sheet accounting, objective data models, and long-form investigative journalism.

1. Our Mission & Editorial Philosophy

Founded in 2024, The Bull Print was established to bridge the information asymmetry between institutional boardrooms and retail market participants. While mainstream financial media often prioritizes speculative noise, intraday tips, and sensationalist headlines, our research desk focuses exclusively on primary source financial verification: audited corporate filings, statutory disclosures, central bank balance sheets, and on-chain ledger forensics.

We do not sell trading courses, subscription tip services, or speculative signals. Every article, video documentary, and financial terminal on our platform is provided entirely free of charge for investor education and analytical research.

2. E-E-A-T & Forensic Methodology

Our editorial process adheres to Google's rigorous Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T) standards:

  • Exchange Sourced Data: All Dalal Street equity profiles draw directly from National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) regulatory submissions.
  • Audited Balance Sheet Forensics: We audit capital structure under Indian Accounting Standards (IndAS), specifically adjusting for lease capitalizations (IndAS-116), promoter pledging, and off-balance sheet guarantees.
  • No AI Hallucinations: All published investigative research passes through multi-tier practitioner reviews, statutory reconciliation, and strict algorithmic quality gates.

3. SEBI Statutory Independence Notice

Statutory Notice: The Bull Print operates as a purely educational and observational financial research platform.
