PC Jeweller Limited is one of the leading jewellery companies in India in the organized jewellery retail sector. The company manufactures, sells, and trades gold jewellery, diamond jewellery, and silver articles across pan-India showroom networks. It has successfully executed a comprehensive One-Time Settlement (OTS) with consortium lenders.

✓ Core Fundamental Catalysts

  • ✓ Company has executed One-Time Settlement (OTS) with 14 consortium banks, removing legacy litigation overhang.
  • ✓ Substantial reduction in debt and promoter warrant equity infusion of ₹1,500 Cr.
  • ✓ Showroom network revival ahead of wedding and festive seasons.

⚠ Structural Headwinds & Risks
