ITC Limited is one of India's foremost private sector companies. A diversified conglomerate commanding ~75% legal cigarette market share alongside market-leading consumer brands (Aashirvaad, Sunfeast, Bingo, Yippee), Agri-Business, Paperboards, and IT. Fortress balance sheet with zero long-term debt.

✓ Core Fundamental Catalysts

  • ✓ Company is virtually debt-free with free cash flow generation exceeding ₹16,000 Cr annually.
  • ✓ Exceptional dividend track record paying out 80-85% of annual profits as dividends (yield 5.56%).
  • ✓ Approved NCLT demerger of Hotels business unlocking capital efficiency.

⚠ Structural Headwinds & Risks

  • ⚠ Paperboards segment facing margin compression due to cheap Asian pulp imports.
  • ⚠ Periodic tobacco excise duty and taxation sensitivity.
