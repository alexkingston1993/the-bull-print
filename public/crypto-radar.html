📊 Indian Section 115BBH & 194S Tax Rules on DEX Trades

Indian tax regulations enacted under the Finance Act 2022 strictly govern all Virtual Digital Asset (VDA) transactions, including decentralized swaps executed through phantom wallets, MetaMask, or smart contracts on Solana and Base:

1. Flat 30% Tax Rate (Section 115BBH): Any gain realized from selling or swapping a token is taxed at a flat rate of 30% (plus a 4% Health & Education cess, reaching an effective rate of 31.2%). Crucially, losses suffered on one token cannot be set off against profits made on another token.

2. 1% TDS Liability (Section 194S): A 1% Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is mandated on any transfer of a virtual digital asset exceeding ₹50,000 (or ₹10,000 for specified persons) annually. In decentralized on-chain environments where no intermediary deducts TDS, the individual trader bears the statutory obligation to self-declare and remit the 1% TDS through Form 26QE.
