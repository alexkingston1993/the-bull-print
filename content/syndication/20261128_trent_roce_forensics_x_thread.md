# X / TWITTER THREAD: Trent Ltd 10-Year Balance Sheet Forensics: ROCE & Debt-to-Equity Trajectory

1/9 🧵 Dalal Street's favorite 100x compounder is Trent Ltd ($TRENT).

Consensus analyst models quote Trent's ROCE at an eye-popping 24% to 28% with a P/E multiple around 140x.

But when you forensic-audit the balance sheet under IndAS-116 (Lease Capitalization), the underlying unit economics tell a very different story 👇

---

2/9 ⚡ The Zudio Velocity Engine:

Zudio doesn't win on high gross margins. It wins on pure inventory turnover:
• 82% of SKUs under ₹499
• ~70 inventory days vs 130 days for traditional retail
• 180+ store additions annually

Because store footprints are leased, IndAS-116 dramatically alters the P&L.

---

3/9 📊 IndAS-116 Accounting Distortions:

Under IndAS-116, operating lease rent is REMOVED from operating expenses.

Instead, you get:
1. Depreciation on 'Right of Use' (ROU) assets
2. Finance charges on Lease Liabilities

This artificially expands reported EBITDA and EBIT by hundreds of crores.

---

4/9 🔍 The 10-Year ROCE Deconstruction:

• FY18 Reported ROCE: 6.8%
• FY22 Reported ROCE: 6.6%
• FY24 Reported ROCE: 20.4%
• FY25 TTM Reported: 24.1%

Now look at un-levered core operating ROCE (stripping out lease financial leverage):
• FY24 Adjusted: 16.2%
• FY25 TTM Adjusted: 18.8%

---

5/9 ⚠️ The Real Balance Sheet Debt:

Look only at bank debt, and Trent appears net-cash positive (<₹500 Cr borrowings).

Factor in non-cancellable store lease liabilities (>₹6,750 Cr), and Trent's economic Debt-to-Equity is 1.85x.

Retail lease commitments don't stop if footfalls slow down.

---

6/9 📉 Operating Leverage Risk:

Zudio is an operating leverage play. A 5% drop in same-store sales (SSSG) translates to a 3.2x multiplier compression in operating margins because lease depreciation is fixed.

At 140x P/E, Dalal Street is pricing in perpetual flawless execution.

---

7/9 🎯 Bottom Line:

Trent is an extraordinary Indian retail execution story. 18.8% adjusted ROCE is still best-in-class for apparel.

However, treating reported 24%+ ROCE as un-levered return on capital ignores ₹6,700 Cr+ in contractual lease debt.
