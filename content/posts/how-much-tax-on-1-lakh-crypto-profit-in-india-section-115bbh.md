Section 194S: The 1% TDS Tracking Mechanism</h2> <p> Section 194S requires Indian exchanges and P2P buyers to withhold 1% TDS on any transfer of a Virtual Digital Asset exceeding ₹50,000 in a financial year (or ₹10,000 for non-specified persons). This TDS is credited to your PAN and directly reconciled in Form 26AS under code <strong>E-28</strong>. </p> <h2>3. Reporting in ITR-2 Schedule VDA</h2> <p> Taxpayers with crypto income must disclose every trade lot in <strong>Schedule VDA</strong> of Form ITR-2 or ITR-3.
